# NYU Robotic Design Team

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Introduce NYU's student Robotic Design Team, its lunar mining robots, its members, and its participation in NASA Lunabotics. Let visitors understand the work and contact the team.

## Users

People interested in the team, its robot projects, and supporting its work. Prioritization between prospective members and sponsors remains unspecified; the approved homepage leads with the team and robot, with contact and sponsorship available directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve the existing discipline-based team roster, previous-year robot breakdown, project links, and news references. Primary navigation is Team, Previous Years, Contact. News belongs on a separate page linked from the footer. Sponsorship belongs in Contact. Replace the scroll-driven explosion, leader lines, and design-variant selector with a simpler presentation. Preserve the static HTML/CSS/JavaScript architecture and existing contact destination. Do not fabricate performance metrics, awards, application deadlines, or sponsor benefits.

## Brand Commitments

NYU Robotic Design Team identity and existing logos. User approved a simpler photography-led direction inspired by CalSol, Michigan Solar Car, Olin Baja, and Olin Electric Motorsports. Purple may serve as the main brand/accent color. The user approved proceeding with this direction and supplied photo locations for the implementation.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing roster at `scripts/lead_info.json`; project history and four publication links in `index.html`; existing media under `resources/`. Additional user-supplied robot photography and renders are in `/run/media/g/3034-6438/` and `/home/g/Downloads/(0) Logos, Photos, Assets/`; related Downloads assets may be inspected. ## Product Principles

- Real engineering work and real imagery establish credibility.
- Team organization and robot history remain easy to browse.
- Navigation and interaction should be straightforward.
- Preserve factual content when replacing presentation.
