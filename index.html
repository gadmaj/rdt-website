Exploded view

Locomotion

Four chain-driven wheel modules and the frame they carry: the rolling base everything mounts to. Built by Mechanical and Electrical Engineering.

Excavation

Bucket-ladder digging system that harvests lunar regolith. Built by Mechanical and Software Engineering.

Deposition

Collects and dumps harvested regolith into the sieve. Built by Mechanical Engineering.

EE Box

Power distribution, motor controllers, and the wiring that animates every subsystem. Built by Electrical Engineering.

Mission Operations

Luna comes back together under the people who run the program: leadership, project management, marketing, and outreach.

00  —  Mission

Mission

We are a team of undergraduate students who design, build, test, and compete a lunar mining robot at NASA’s annual Lunabotics competition, all while advancing autonomous excavation research and bringing robotics outreach across New York City.

01  —  Roster

Team

A vertically integrated project: mechanical, electrical, and software disciplines assembled around one robot, one competition season at a time. Members are listed by role, not by name.

02  —  Build Record

Projects
